Kodu

In AICT, we made types of games which we can choose what to make during the unit of game development. During this unit we used a software called Kodu to make a game of our choice as our final project for the year. I used the patterns Health, Score, Big boss, In game information as my patterns in the game I made. The patterns are important is because the patterns are what make the mechanics in the game we built. I think the 2 things I should improve on is making more patterns and improve on the map I created.
